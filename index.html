One cycle, never the same twice.

heat · rise · cool
01 / heat

The pool warms

A bulb sits under the bottle. The wax at the bottom is a shade denser than the liquid until it warms through, and a few degrees is enough to tip the balance.

02 / rise

A blob lets go

Expanded wax is lighter than the liquid around it, so a body pinches off the pool and climbs, stretching into a teardrop as it goes. The trip up takes half a minute or more.

03 / cool

It stalls and sinks

Away from the bulb the blob sheds heat, hangs near the shoulder of the glass, then drops back into the pool to melt again. The bodies never line up, which is the whole point.
